Maternal & Child Healthcare

Antenatal Care (ANC) Schedule

1

Before 12 Weeks

Pregnancy confirmation, Hb & blood group, BP check, TT-1, IFA tablets, register at PHC

2

14–26 Weeks

Anomaly ultrasound, weight check, urine test, TT-2 or booster, dietary counselling

3

28–34 Weeks

Foetal growth monitoring, Hb recheck, hospital birth plan discussion

4

34–40 Weeks

Final preparation, danger sign education, referral transport arrangement (JSSK)

Danger Signs – Go to Hospital NOW:

Heavy bleeding • Severe headache • Blurred vision • Reduced fetal movement • High fever • Fits • Labour before 37 weeks

Postnatal Care (PNC)

  • ✅ First PNC within 24 hours of delivery
  • ✅ 3 more visits: day 3, day 7, day 42
  • ✅ Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months
  • ✅ Vitamin A supplementation
  • ✅ Family planning counselling

Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

  • ✅ Cash incentive for institutional delivery
  • ✅ Rural mother: ₹1,400 | Urban: ₹1,000
  • ✅ ASHA worker: ₹300–600 per delivery
  • ✅ Contact your nearest ASHA/ANM worker

Child Nutrition Milestones

  • • Birth–6 months: Exclusive breastfeeding only
  • • 6 months: Start mashed dal, rice, banana
  • • 9 months: Vitamin A dose at PHC
  • • Monthly weighing at Anganwadi
  • • MUAC <11.5 cm → NRC admission

PMMVY – Maternity Benefit

₹5,000 in 3 installments for first live birth. Directly credited to mother's bank account.

• Register with ASHA within 150 days of LMP

• Aadhaar + Bank account required

• Apply at nearest Anganwadi centre

Family Planning (Mission Parivar Vikas)

Free contraceptives & sterilisation services.

✅ Free condoms, oral pills, Copper-T

✅ Injectable MPA (Antara programme)

✅ Sterilisation with ₹1,400 compensation

Helpline: 1800-111-6556
